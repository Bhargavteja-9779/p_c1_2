# Highlights

- Standard 90% intervals covered only 50.9% of the most expensive house sales
- Even oracle covariate-conditional intervals under-cover rare outcomes
- Stratum-wise density level sets are the smallest stratum-valid prediction sets
- SDLC gave the smallest stratum-valid sets on average, replicated on held-out data
- Decision study compares set-based tail alerts with conformal tail classifiers
