  2026-01-01 (script `measure/crossref_times.py`; full JKSU-CIS census `measure/jksu_full.py` → `measure/jksu_cis_2026.csv`).
  For OUP (no Crossref assertions), dates were read from 24 JCDE article pages via doi.org.
* Official pages: https://link.springer.com/journal/44443 (JIF 6.4 (2025), 5-year 6.0, indexing list incl. SCIE),
  /submission-guidelines, /how-to-publish-with-us (APC, OA model). Clarivate JCR/MJL not reachable (login/JS).
* Quartile: King Saud University news item, 29 June 2025, https://news.ksu.edu.sa/en/node/145338 (Q1, JCR 2024).
* Integrity: searches for "on hold", delisting, paper-mill or mass-retraction reports for this title returned none;
  not on the June 2025 Clarivate suppression list reported by Retraction Watch; Crossref update records show two
  withdrawn articles, one batch of 2020 errata (missing competing-interest statements) and routine corrigenda.
  Live MJL status should be confirmed by the authors at https://mjl.clarivate.com before submission.

## Distinctions respected
The 69-day figure is received→accepted for papers that were eventually accepted; it is neither time to first
decision nor time to publication, and it excludes rejected manuscripts. The journal publishes no official timing
statistics. SciRev (editor-provided, year unknown): 23 % acceptance, 35 % desk rejection — unverified.
