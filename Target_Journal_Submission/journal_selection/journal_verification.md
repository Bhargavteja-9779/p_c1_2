# Journal verification log (2026-11-15)

Method. Official publisher pages were fetched where possible. ScienceDirect (Elsevier) and IEEE pages blocked automated
access (HTTP 403 / 418); for Elsevier, values were taken from shop.elsevier.com (JIF) and search-engine snapshots of the
official ScienceDirect pages (timelines). Springer and OUP pages were read directly. Clarivate JCR requires
institutional login and was not accessible: **all JCR quartiles are secondary-source unless stated**.
Independent acceptance-time measurement: median received→accepted interval computed from dates deposited in
Crossref (Springer, IOP, T&F, Wiley, ACM, IET) or PubMed (OUP; Elsevier medical titles), articles dated 2026-01-01..2026-11-15.
Elsevier non-medical titles do not deposit these dates, so no independent measurement was possible for ESWA.

Distinctions respected: submission→first decision (includes desk rejections) ≠ submission→acceptance ≠ acceptance→online.
