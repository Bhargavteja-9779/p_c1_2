## Constraint that could NOT be satisfied
The user's requirement of a verified submission-to-final-acceptance time ≤ 100 days (initially ≤ 72 days) could
not be satisfied by any reputable Q1-candidate journal with suitable scope among ~30 examined
(see journal_comparison.md). No publisher page or measured Crossref/PubMed sample showed a median ≤ 100 days for a
suitable journal. Following the instruction "select the strongest scientifically defensible Q1 JCR candidate and
explicitly identify which requirement could not be verified or satisfied", ESWA was chosen because it combines
(a) the highest JIF among journals with genuine topic fit, (b) one of the shortest reported acceptance times among
high-JIF candidates (147 d snapshot), (c) demonstrated publication of both conformal prediction and imbalanced
regression research. **No acceptance-time guarantee is implied.**
